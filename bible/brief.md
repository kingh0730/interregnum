# The brief (King, 2026-09-29)

Create a series of short films, kind of like *Love, Death & Robots*, to prove the best filmmaking possible.

Tell the story of our generation: people at the end of an era, confused, excited, tired, some cynical, some hopeful.

**Possible angles:** AI and robots (with all their ramifications); AI and human consciousness; social media;
pandemics; war; China (including China's own narrative); Trump, Xi, Deng, Mao, Putin, etc.; ideologies and
religions; Sam Altman, Elon Musk, etc.; love and family; …

**Rule of the world:** fictional. Places, times, stories, names, appearances and possibly genders are all altered.

**Ambition:** told so that it could become a blockbuster, the way these works did.

**Loved works:** Attack on Titan · The Three-Body Problem · Interstellar · Tenet · The Odyssey (Christopher Nolan) ·

## Series title: AI SI - I (2026-10-02)

The public-facing name is **AI SI - I**. Preserve that capitalization, spacing and literal `-` character.
**INTERREGNUM / interregnum** remains an internal working name and the repository name.

The title deliberately supports several readings; these are overlapping meanings, not one definitive decoding:

- **Love and death:** AI and SI evoke Mandarin *ài* / *sǐ*, 爱 / 死, acknowledging the inspiration of
  *LOVE DEATH + ROBOTS*. The `-` also answers the original's `+`.
- **Artificial intelligence and super intelligence:** AI and SI sit alongside each other as names for intelligence.
  The contemporary naming context is the September 29, 2026 US executive order directing the executive branch
  to use “Super Intelligence” / “SI” in place of “Artificial Intelligence” / “AI” in official materials.
  See [the executive order](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/09/inaugurating-the-era-of-super-intelligence/).
  Naming intelligence, and the power to rename it, is another layer of the title.
- **Subtraction:** read the letters as `AISI - I = ASI`, yielding artificial superintelligence by removing an “I”.
  This allows a reading of the self or human viewpoint disappearing as something larger emerges.
- **A dash:** the same `-` can be punctuation—a pause, break, connection or introduction of a voice.
  “AI, SI— I” can leave the self present, asserting itself or beginning an unfinished sentence.
- **Combination:** AI and SI can also be read as coming together into “I”—an intelligence or a self.
  The final “I” can be an outcome, not only something subtracted. This is an open possibility, not a fixed equation.
- **The interval:** the gap can echo the interregnum, the space between an ending and what comes next.

Keep the ambiguity alive: disappearance and emergence, separation and combination can coexist.
These notes preserve the creative readings; audience-facing material need not explain them all.
