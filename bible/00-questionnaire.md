# Questionnaire for King

Answer in any order, as briefly or as long as you like; gut reactions are as useful as essays. Skip anything
that doesn't spark. Your answers shape the bible and the episode slate.

**Write your answers in `bible/private/answers.md`** (gitignored). This repo is public, so candid answers,
especially political ones, stay out of git. The committed bible will hold only the creative choices built on them.

## The generation
1. **Whose generation?** Roughly which ages, and where: China, the US, the diaspora, everywhere? Whose eyes
   should the series mostly look through?
2. **Confused, excited, tired, cynical, hopeful:** which do you feel most, day to day? Which should the whole
   series *end* on?
3. **A moment** when you personally felt "this is the end of an era." Small and specific is best.

## The angles
4. **Rank them:** which angles do you feel most strongly about? Which would hurt most if I got them wrong?
5. **AI:** threat, mirror, child, god, coworker, lover, successor? What's your honest feeling, including about
   an AI making this series?
6. **China and its own narrative:** which parts matter most (reform and opening, the rise, 996, involution,
   lying flat, zero-COVID, the diaspora, national rejuvenation)? Tell it from the inside, the outside, or both?
   Any red lines?
7. **The leaders and tech figures** (Trump, Xi, Deng, Mao, Putin, Altman, Musk…): satire in the style of *House of
   Cards*, myth in the style of *Dune* or *Game of Thrones*, or tragedy in the style of *Joker*? Anyone you want
   treated with particular empathy, or particular bite? Should each be clearly one character, or should figures
   be blended?
8. **War and pandemic:** how close to real events (COVID and lockdowns, Ukraine, the Middle East, the Taiwan
   Strait)? Anything off-limits?
9. **Love and family:** which kind? The gap between parents and children, marriage and birth-rate pressure,
   long distance, AI companions, friendship, loneliness?

## The works you love
10. **Pick your 5 closest** and name the exact moment or feeling that hooks you, for example Tenet's
    palindrome reveal, Your Name's comet, or the basement in Attack on Titan.
11. **Clusters:** I read your list as roughly:
    - (a) cosmic scale and time: Interstellar, Tenet, Arrival, Three-Body, The Wandering Earth, Aquila Rift
    - (b) power games: Game of Thrones, House of Cards, The Hunger Games, Dune
    - (c) monstrous and changed bodies: Attack on Titan, Parasyte, Tokyo Ghoul, Ajin, Alita
    - (d) minds and machines: Ex Machina, Psycho-Pass, Blade Runner 2049, Astro Boy
    - (e) aching romance: Your Name, Weathering with You, Eternal Sunshine
    - (f) warmth and home: Stardew Valley, Minecraft, Frozen, Spy × Family, Big Hero 6, Last Christmas, Superman

    Is that right? Which clusters *must* be in the series? And what do the cozy ones (Stardew, Minecraft, Last
    Christmas) mean to you in this list?
12. **What you dislike**, even in works you love (endings, tropes, pacing, preachiness…).
13. **Tell me about *All Wishes Come True!* (2026) and the *Lord of Mysteries* game trailer.** I may know little or
    nothing about them, and I don't want to guess what you love in them.

## Format
14. **Scale:** how many episodes, how long each (*Love, Death & Robots* runs 6–17 minutes), and should we start
    with one pilot to prove the pipeline?
15. **Language:** English, Chinese, mixed, or mostly wordless? Subtitles?
16. **Look:** a different medium per episode, like *Love, Death & Robots* (anime, painterly, 3D, photoreal)? Any
    style you don't want?
17. **Connection and endings:** standalone episodes, a hidden shared universe, or a finale that ties them
    together? Hopeful, bittersweet or devastating?
18. **Audience and budget:** who is it for (you, friends, a public YouTube or Bilibili release), and what's
    your ceiling on video-model spending? A public release changes how carefully real figures must be
    fictionalized.
