# This episode's bible

The choices that belong to this film alone, written under the series rules in `../../../bible/visual.md`,
`../../../bible/sound.md` and `../../../bible/taste.md` (`docs/playbook.md` §2 and §6):

- `art_direction.md`: the medium, its style block, and the episode's copy
- `production_design.md`: what things are, and the palette's meanings
- `cinematography.md`: whose eye, lens and height, composition, the camera's home register, its moves and its one
  surprise
- `test_frames.md`: the test prompts for choosing the direction
- `sound.md`: the episode's voices, rooms, motifs and music spotting

The pilot's bible (`../../pilot/bible/`) is a worked example, not a rulebook.
