## The image we are making

A magnificent maritime future that people can inhabit without ceremony. Photographic Chinese adults, couture with clean functional construction, enormous but intelligible engineering, open sea and hard beautiful daylight. The lovers are the emotional subject; the city makes their small choice feel large. Full 16:9 at 1920×1080 delivery. No inherited letterbox.

The future is visible in the building's **shape and function**, rather than adjectives about luxury. The approved revised master has enormous hollow tower forms across the bay, three open oval floors, thin skeletal arches, a permanent broad pedestrian approach and machine guide rails carrying the touring decks on visible bogies. Pearl surfaces, dark blue glass and luminous water carry the beauty. Large negative spaces between main forms are more valuable than additional ornamental detail.

The initial porcelain campus frame and the first enclosed-saucer world are not approved masters. The current world image is an **oblique view across a bay**, not the common sea-facing dance camera. Background buildings therefore do not dictate a city-behind-sea layout in every shot. Local master angles establish screen-space lighting, skyline visibility and rail position for their own view.

The setting has three levels of detail: one spectacular silhouette in the distance, three readable story spaces in the middle, and the specific person or object in the foreground. A skyline may be extraordinary without filling every gap. Give the dance an uncluttered floor and the eye one place to land. More greebles, fins or glowing strips will not repair weak architecture.

## Choice among the three tested directions

| Direction | Beauty source | Risk to judge |
|---|---|---|
| Porcelain — selected and developed | Pearl ceramic, silver ribs, cobalt glass; bright directional sun makes precise shapes | Initial office-estate massing was rejected. The revised open-water master supplies the missing future shape and function. |
| Prism | Clear structural glass, dark titanium and pale stone; one restrained violet refraction | Facets can become decorative clutter, a game environment or noisy skin reflections. |
| Nocturne | Basalt, luminous white ceramic and one coral architectural emitter against indigo sky | The main source can flatten faces into red, hide feet or turn the world into a generic neon stage. |

Porcelain is the home direction throughout. Prism and nocturne are retained as comparison tests, not chorus treatments. Vary shot size, sun exposure and the proportion of sea to architecture within the selected family. Keep one continuous lighting period, with local angles following their approved source direction.

## Bodies and materials

Lin's ivory high-neck sleeveless top, wide straight trousers, flat ivory shoes and left-ear silver cuff remain fixed. Yu's ink-blue collarless jacket, grey shirt, tailored trousers and dark flat shoes remain fixed. The approved portraits are original fictional adult identities. Luma scene work references the original bases; no chain of successive face edits. Check age, bob length, ear cuff side, hairline and clear complexion alongside likeness.

Beauty is a deliberate priority. Do not add blotches, age lines, greasy skin, artificial skin smoothing, oversized eyes or bright beauty-filter sparkles to make the faces seem photographic. Faces remain composed; pleasure comes through attention, proximity and movement. Bodies may be energetic in dance while the face stays small. No mouthing the recording.

Photorealism is the medium throughout. Anime informs silhouette, the held anticipatory pose and the cut that completes a movement. Cyberpunk informs visible power, guide mechanisms and tailored conductive seams. Neither licenses cartoon skin or a floating graphic interface. The physical pendulum demonstration is the episode's **copy**: schematic symmetry is concentrated in this deliberately manufactured model.
