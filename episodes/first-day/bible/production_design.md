# 第一天 — production design

Authority: `episodes/first-day/build/director_lock.md`. Material/light choice remains provisional until the director compares the six look tests. **A, B and I below are fixed story spaces**, not independent visual inventions in every prompt.

## A place to wait

The sea is south and the city is north. Two mechanically supported touring galleries follow separate guided circuits past a fixed public island. They dock there at different times. The public path connects the island to land throughout. Motion along the gallery routes does not change the island's shape, support or orientation.

The following diagram is a continuity plan, not an engineering drawing. The common camera looks south; screen left and right below are screen coordinates, not compass directions.

| Set | Permanent identity | Geography anchors |
|---|---|---|
| A — Lin's gallery | Pearl ceramic floor, one substantial ivory arch, cobalt curved glass sea rail, low ivory bench | Arch and bench on inland side; docking threshold has an apricot strip; guide mechanism below the solid deck |
| B — Yu's gallery | Blue-grey floor, slim dark titanium arch, clear blue sea rail, dark bench | Arch and bench inland; one vertical coral inset identifies reverse views; guide mechanism below deck |
| I — fixed island | Oval pearl floor, three silver ribs on its north side, broad open centre | Two flanking docks; permanent inland path; south sea rail; a low bench kept outside the dance area |

The final material choice may shift finish and source light but must retain these shape/value distinctions. A should be recognizable without Lin; B without Yu; I without either. Do not use colour alone to communicate occupancy or safety.

## Docking states

Each flank has a broad single-piece transfer connection with glass side rails and a gate. A connection is traversable only when the relevant gallery is stopped, the floor is level and the gate open. For the still reel, show these states in separate clear pictures. Do not ask a generated shot to perform a complex telescoping bridge transformation behind a crossing actor.

1. **Passing:** gallery on its visible guide path; no open route across the water.
2. **Docked:** deck and island aligned, bridge surface continuous, gate open. Establish this before any foot crosses.
3. **Departure:** the person who stays is already entirely on I; connection is closed before the gallery recedes.

The optional retracting boarding ribbon is a small physical furnishing, not a route-display system. Its movement may punctuate departure, but the gallery itself must carry that fact. No explanatory sign or numerical countdown is required.

No failed island visits or reciprocal retreats before 160 seconds. The two missed connections are hesitations aboard. **The change is that someone finally stays**, not that either person solves a transport puzzle.

## The future outside the plot

The guide rails, support pylons and joints are functional scale cues, visible in selected wide views. The spectacular skyline should have one dominant structural idea and air between masses. Do not repeat the rejected campus's horizontal office-window grid across the horizon. Keep the sea present where the camera plan allows; it is the open view that the pair eventually shares.

Footwear, two benches and the gate hardware are the useful intimate objects. Rain residue may occur at Lin's rail in the first verse; it does not require a wet world. Hair and one jacket edge respond to the same gentle breeze. Avoid constantly blowing large costume panels across hands or faces.

The optional tabletop **copy** is the approved physical phase model: sixteen polished bobs hanging in a graded row beneath a metal support. Preserve that object, its supports and scale across shots. It is adopted as a fictional adaptation of Harvard's fifteen-pendulum demonstration, not an exact reconstruction; do not transfer the laboratory device's oscillation counts to it. This set dressing echoes asynchronous arrivals without becoming an explanation the viewer must decode. Its laboratory inspiration does not validate the gallery engineering.

## Master-reference checklist

Approve a common geography view before individual angles. Preserve the same arches, docks, ribs, access path, sea level and sun direction. Each later scene uses the relevant A/B/I reference plus the original face base. Judge the starting frames together in edit order: attractive isolated images do not compensate for a dock switching sides or an island becoming a fourth gallery.
