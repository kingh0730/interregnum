## Dramatic continuity

Opening: a shoe/palm match appears to make a pair; wide reveals separate galleries. First verse: they notice each other's phrase and attempt an approach. First chorus: apparent duet constructed by edits, faces and all bodies visible. Second verse: benches, wind, an imperfect imitation; actual scenes with no dance display. End of first chorus: Lin hesitates at her stationary dock and stays aboard. Second chorus: Yu repeats that hesitation at his dock while Lin passes away. Neither steps onto the island until Lin does so at 2:40–2:44. No complex maze. At 2:44 bodies and camera stop. Lin stays on the fixed island as her empty gallery leaves. At 2:59 she gives the familiar phrase but omits its retreating final step. Yu reaches her. The final chorus holds them together through the completion of the phrase. They end side by side, looking outward; no explanatory moral or obligatory kiss.

**Phrase:** grounded heel touch → quarter turn → open palm → backward step. Early cuts borrow each other's action. Final phrase changes only the backward step: Lin waits, Yu enters, and they finish together. The eventual motion must show complete phrases in a few sustained full-body shots; the story reel cannot prove choreography, contact or beat synchronization.

## Shot and audio policy

Preserve full original recording with one static gain only. Chinese subtitles are screen-space, with no English translation required for this Mandarin edition. Supplied English refrain remains as sung. Credits do not replace or overlap the closing lyric.

Prepare neutral, balanced anticipatory startframes. Where contact or a crossing matters, supply a separate landing/end pose and record the required visible action. Never claim a still verifies movement. No paid video-generation submission during this production.
