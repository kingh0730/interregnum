The extracted paths are not redrawn or reshaped. Source SVGs and raster working copies live in
`work/first-day-anime-test/assets/`. Downloaded 2026-10-03. These are brand assets, not an assertion of sponsorship.

https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl . SIL OFL texts are saved alongside the font binaries.

Meme review: search evidence confirms established Bilibili vocabulary, rather than establishing that every
phrase is a current trend. Keep the director's playful anime-community idiom. `awsl`, `爷青回` and `yyds`
are intentionally nostalgic staples, not newly coined slang. No new topical claims or capability claims added.
`你说得对` remains the scripted fictional reply; the search was insufficient to substantiate the plan's
superlative “most iconic” claim, so production notes do not repeat that claim.
