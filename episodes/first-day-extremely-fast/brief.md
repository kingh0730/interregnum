# 《第一天》／极快篇：创作约定

**Series:** AI SI - I  
**Commission:** 2026-10-02. A Mandarin music pilot about the birth of AIs. The immediate commission ends at art direction, including actual look tests.  
**Priority:** exceptional visual invention, beauty and extremely fast, varied audiovisual rhythm. Technique must be visible in the result and necessary to the action.

## Source and scope

- Use only this episode's supplied `first-day.wav` and `first-day.lrc`. The WAV measures **43.670 seconds**, 1,925,847 samples per channel, 44,100 Hz stereo, 24-bit PCM. Preserve its bytes and speed.
- Read the existing series guidance and shared memories. Other First Day episodes are excluded as creative, image, script, timing and production references. No material is imported from them.
- Chinese is the audience language. Chinese story text and legible Chinese lyric treatment; actual product names may retain their familiar Latin spelling. No new explanatory voice-over, translated song or additional verse.
- Proposed final canvas: **16:9, 3840 × 2160, 60 fps**. The audio ends at 43.670; 2,621 video frames end at 43.683333. Only the 13.333 ms conform tail may be padded. These are production intentions, not a rendered-master claim.
- Deliver concept development and independent critique, dated research, timed script and shot plans, scene-state plan, three contrasting directions tested with two fresh frames each, selected visual bibles, image review/provenance and a clear handoff. Further selected-direction studies are allowed where they resolve a real visual question.
- Full keyframe production, a story reel, soundtrack production and generated motion belong to later stages. The current budget covers a small art-direction image study only.

## Episode-specific choices under the series rules

The commission explicitly replaces the usual 3–5-minute dramatic-film length and 2–10-second shot template. Short cut cells can be a few frames; causal actions need enough time to read across those cells. A technique inventory alone is not a story.

The named AI products are part of the requested fiction and Chinese meme culture. They can have playful fictional manifestations here. This does not authorize invented factual product failures, real-person likenesses, geopolitical combat, or claims of company endorsement. No existing first-day project is used to interpret the commission.

The soundtrack is the supplied song. Its measured low-energy trough around **11.4–11.9 seconds**, followed by the strong transient near **11.92**, supplies a natural break. Visual stillness must also be designed elsewhere; the music does not need to be cut to create it. Measured periodicity candidates are **88.43 / 176.87 BPM**. They are a half/double-time interpretation, not approved musical metre or exact vocal alignment.

Multiple media are required by the brief and must obey a rule established early: the same object, state or action can acquire another material language, while its identity, count and direction remain accountable. A random style filter is not a motivated medium change.

The technical timeline, exact labels and composited physical graphics will be authored. Original environments follow the existing Luma default; elaborate attention-commanding focal designs and exploratory invention can use Codex image generation under playbook §2b. Generative edits use Codex. Every test remains an art study until actual production-state review.

## Acceptance questions

1. Can a viewer retell the action after one viewing without reading a production note?
2. Is the first half-second an arresting physical situation, with a consequence by two seconds?
3. Do major AI systems visibly participate rather than exist only in a credit list? Record a bounded, dated coverage roster.
4. Are recent Chinese memes dated and sourced, and are older ones identified honestly? A meme is a visible action before it is a label.
5. Do AlphaGo and Transformer history affect the action? Future AGI, SGI and singularity remain speculation, without fictional achievement dates presented as fact.
6. Does speed change in cutting, object motion, camera behavior, light, colour, scale, sound density and emotional pressure? Include a genuine stop and an elegant held image.
7. Does each effect have a source, a material, a force, a consequence and an exit? Particles cannot run unchanged over the whole film.
8. Are the functional insert and quiet image as art-directed as the hero spectacle?
9. What evidence supports comparison with the benchmark? Design ambition and attractive stills do not prove superior finished motion.
10. Are the source, actual prompts, output versions, review limits and next-stage dependencies explicit?
