## What the evidence can support

The clearest 2026 material is the movement from talking to **catching, repeating, eating tokens and doing work**. Turn these into events involving original physical objects. A recognisable phrase earns at most one beat; it is not the plot. In 43.670 seconds, five or six legible actions will carry more than twenty equally weighted references.

Confidence labels distinguish the existence of a joke from claims about its reach:

- **A — opened primary text:** the actual community post, project or official source was retrieved. This establishes what the author posted, not that a screenshot is an unedited model output or that the audience is representative.
- **B — indexed primary page:** a platform's own indexed page exposed title, date and sometimes engagement, but direct retrieval/playback was blocked. Dates and visible counts are recorded as indexed metadata, not independently audited metrics.
- **C — secondary lead:** useful only for further checking. It does not establish a trend and is not enough for a specific factual claim in the film.

All counts are snapshots of the retrieved/indexed representation. No source here establishes that a meme was the first of its kind, reached all Mandarin speakers, or is a model's stable current behaviour. The film's AI creatures are affectionate personifications, not product tests.

## Future concepts: preserve uncertainty as an image property

**AGI / 通用人工智能:** use broad capability across tasks as the accessible idea. One influential framework separates **performance, generality and autonomy** rather than announcing a single magical threshold. This is a proposal, not a universally accepted test. [*Levels of AGI*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.02462), first posted November 4, 2023; revised September 24, 2025. A creature using one body to solve several different physical problems is clearer than a glowing brain labelled AGI.

**ASI / 超级人工智能:** the same framework places broadly superhuman capability at the top of its proposed performance/generality matrix. Its model-status examples are dated to the paper; do not use them to certify the status of October 2026 systems. ASI can appear as a speculative empty outline that exceeds the room's current scale. [Primary framework, §4](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.02462v5).

**SGI is not a single agreed abbreviation and must not silently become ASI.** At least three primary uses exist:

| Expansion actually used | Source and date | Implication for our film |
|---|---|---|
| **Specialized Generalist AI** / SGAI or SGI | [Zhang, Qi and Zhou](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.08642), July 11, 2024: exceptional specialisation while retaining general ability. | This is not simply a level above AGI. |
| **Scientific General Intelligence** | [Xu et al., SGI-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16969), December 18, 2025: scientist-aligned inquiry across domains. | This is an operational research definition, not a universal name for superintelligence. |
| **Super-general-intelligence** | [Fred Shone's own essay](https://fredshone.github.io/blog/2026/AGI-vs-SGI-HGI/), March 19, 2026. | Establishes one author's usage, not consensus. |

**Recommended on-screen handling:** keep the user's **SGI** as an unfinished speculative name, such as a physical tag reading **SGI？**, inside a future branch. If a script later spells it out, declare which meaning the fictional world uses; do not present **AGI → SGI → singularity** as a scientific ladder. A three-way split with different unfinished shapes makes ambiguity visible without a definition lecture.

**奇点 / technological singularity:** use a speculative threshold beyond which forecasting becomes radically difficult. Vinge's primary essay describes several possible routes to greater-than-human intelligence, rather than one required software upgrade. [1993 essay record and abstract at NASA NTRS](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19940022856). It is a hypothesis about technological change, not a verified scheduled event or a literal astronomical singularity. A horizon that keeps moving as the camera approaches is a better translation than a date-countdown or an inevitable explosion.

## Handoff rules for writing and art direction

1. Build one fictional physical problem that gives the ensemble a reason to interact. A technical reference should change the action, material or camera access; otherwise it is decoration.
2. Reserve the boldest density burst for a shared event. Give one or two jokes enough stable screen time to be recognised. If every reference requires subtitles, reduce the roster's foreground count.
3. Text should be clean Mandarin when prose is necessary. Actual product names can be small durable marks on props. Avoid app screenshots, UI reconstructions, fabricated chat transcripts and CEO likenesses.
4. Preserve **AlphaGo before Transformer**, **Kimi distinct from 哈基米**, **Clawd distinct from OpenClaw**, **product distinct from model**, and **SGI explicitly unresolved**.
5. The freshest opened Chinese community evidence here is the September 6 豆包 reuse; the freshest indexed creative example is September 28 Clawd. March Gemini and February ChatGPT material is attested 2026 culture, not claimed to be this week's new trend. The 2025 DeepSeek catchphrase and 2023/24 Grok persona remain explicitly older.
6. Before production, recheck only selected mutable names and any B-confidence meme whose exact wording or visual design would become central. The present evidence supports original affectionate actions; it does not authorise copying fan assets or reproducing unviewed videos.
