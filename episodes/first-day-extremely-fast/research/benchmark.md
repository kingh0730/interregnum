# Visual benchmark: pdoom-video

Research date: 2026-10-02. Reference: [mexicat/pdoom-video](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video), pinned to [`bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181`](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/tree/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181). No other First Day episode was consulted.

The useful bar is **a succession of legible visual ideas whose construction changes with the music, held together by an exact graphic language**. This is a substantial benchmark. Adding more cuts, more logos, more bloom or more particles would not establish that this episode exceeds it. Our strongest opportunity is a broader experience of materials and scale, with each AI's birth causing a different visible event and the Mandarin language participating in the construction of the world.

This document sets requirements for art direction. It does not claim that our unfinished film exceeds the benchmark's finished motion.

## Construction principles established in source

The treatment gives one travelling motif several identities. It also revisits scene families with changed intensity and uses light/dark inversion to separate passages. Those decisions explain why the large inventory does not read as unrelated demonstrations. Our episode needs its own conserved thing, with a different dramatic purpose; adopting the benchmark's orange travelling line, gridded treatise, probability meter or end-loop would copy its identity. [Treatment](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/docs/TREATMENT.md).

The timeline finds sung phrases and places section boundaries on a nearby beat at or before their first word. Individual scenes then use word starts, word progress, drum events and envelopes. A separate pitch trace is sampled at 100 Hz for one musical deformation. This is an unusually specific relationship between sound and picture: a high note, a consonant, a snare and a section boundary can own different visual actions. A single amplitude value making everything pulse would lose that hierarchy. [Timeline](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/timeline.ts), [audio API](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/engine/audio.ts), [pitch trace](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/scenes/fuse-pitch.ts).

The source goes beyond camera labels. The loss scene builds geometry with a shared scale between its chart and terrain; the room calculates viewpoints, projected planes and flying objects in a common space. Text is arranged with font metrics, kerning and per-glyph positions. These are reasons its high-speed design can be controlled, rather than evidence that any particular render already reads perfectly. [Loss scene](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/scenes/loss.ts), [room scene](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/scenes/room.ts), [type engine](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/engine/type.ts).

Post-production is also specific: HDR compositing, a bloom pyramid, warm halation, a highlight shoulder, edge colour separation, grain and vignette. These share parameters across scenes but allow local overrides. The latest engine averages multiple time samples before the final post pass and chooses a higher sample count when the picture has not converged. This is why clean motion, edge sampling and finishing must be designed with the imagery. It is not a reason to give every one of our materials the same glow or grain. [Post](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/engine/post.ts), [engine](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/app/src/engine/engine.ts), [engine guide](https://github.com/mexicat/pdoom-video/blob/bdbad537a7b7af3213475651774030c47568c181/docs/ENGINE.md).

## Scorecard for an episode with greater visual ambition

Use four evidence states for each row: **specified → shown in selected stills → demonstrated in a short motion test → approved in the complete cut**. A list of techniques earns only the first state. At art-direction delivery, the relevant claims must stop at the stills column; timing and motion remain later work.

| Dimension | What the benchmark establishes | Our target, concretely | What proves the target at art direction | What still requires motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Material beauty** | Consistent engraved/graphic surfaces and careful hot highlights, with a paper inversion. | Let birth change what matter can do: brittle shell, soft fibre, suspended liquid, polished interior and rough fracture should have distinct light responses. At least one frame should be beautiful without legible labels or visible effects. | A hero object and a functional insert, each with an identifiable material, source light and an uncluttered silhouette; no uniform wet or metallic finish. | Material stability during deformation, secondary movement, contact and reflection continuity. |
| **Scale and spatial invention** | Flat charts become deep spaces; rooms, canyons and arrays have real perspective. | A connected journey through at least three perceptibly different scales, such as a microscopic fracture, an object large enough to handle, and an architectural birth chamber. Preserve a repeated shape or action so the jump is legible. | A matched trio of compositions with scale cues and consistent handoff geometry, not three unrelated wide views. | The exact threshold crossing, occlusion and direction through the move. |
| **Range of media** | Several idioms share line work, colour and typography. | Commit to materially different ways of seeing: photographed object, authored drawing/print and controlled three-dimensional transformation. Establish a rule for crossing between them early enough to make the range feel authored. | Side-by-side frames that could not be mistaken for the same render with a filter; the same conserved object/action remains identifiable. | Whether the medium change feels motivated and whether identities survive it. |
| **Particles with meaning** | Sparks burn, geometry branches, objects replicate and glyphs disperse. | At least three causally different particle events: deposition builds a boundary, fracture releases stored matter, and aggregation assembles a new entity. Give each a source, force, destination and residue. | Before/after states plus an annotated active frame. The aftermath must differ from the start. | Weight, collisions, density over time, readable direction and whether the result lands on the intended accent. |
| **Mandarin and identity** | English lyric typography is integrated with scene mechanics. | Chinese writing should have a designed physical life: strokes can be stamped, split, woven or revealed as the new entities arrive. Brand names remain instantly identifiable; meme recognition rewards a second viewing rather than carrying the plot. | Actual Chinese copy at intended mobile size, selected type/letterform treatment and a name-to-object map. Test long and short names together. | Onset accuracy, reading time and whether transformation obscures a name before recognition. |
| **Rhythmic precision** | Phrase-derived boundaries, word/glyph control, separate drum and pitch signals. | Assign separate owners to lyric attack, bass weight, percussion subdivision and phrase change. Use very short bursts inside a larger shape that also has a genuine hold and a stop. The existing song stays the governing clock. | A frame-numbered timing map with event owners; no guessed lyric timings presented as measured facts. | Sample/frame alignment in the final export, musical feel, and whether fast passages remain comprehensible. |
| **Cinematographic contrast** | Falls, rolls, whips, low angles and spatial lettering are implemented. | Most camera motion follows the birth action; one sequence exceeds human scale, and one removes camera motion completely. Change lens, camera height and plane arrangement as well as velocity. | Three-plane staging in spatial frames; a purposeful fixed frame; a diagram of the major impossible move and its landing composition. | Acceleration, shutter, landing stability and readable object continuity through the move. |
| **Meaning and recall** | A repeated motif links concepts and an escalating number structures the whole. | A viewer should recall one birth rule, one consequence and three images after a single pass, even if they cannot name every AI. The history/future/meme material must participate in that rule. | A six-image mute sequence that communicates the cause and result; concept and story agree about what changed. | Whether density overwhelms the hook, and whether the ending changes how the beginning is understood. |
| **Finish and delivery** | Native high-resolution rendering, colour management and substantial temporal sampling are built in. | Protect fine Chinese strokes, particles and saturated highlights through the actual delivery codec; different media may need different finishing. | Full-size and phone-size still checks; one clear focal region per image; planned typography and overlay ownership. | Compression, shimmer, banding, unintended flashing and sample-to-sample motion quality. |

The most valuable test is a paired **spectacle frame and ordinary bridge frame** in each proposed direction. A beautiful climax beside an empty transitional picture would not beat this reference's consistency. The stills should be judged first without knowing which production method made them.

For the supplied song, the episode's audio analysis reports 43.670 seconds and a provisional 176.87 BPM pulse, with a low-energy trough at approximately 11.4–11.9 seconds followed by a strong transient at 11.92. These are the new episode's measurements, not benchmark measurements. Its duration is about 28% of the reference's, so matching the reference's number of distinct scenes would be a poor target. Use the trough for a conspicuous reset in the visual dials and let internal transformations provide density within fewer coherent passages; final beat and lyric alignment remains part of the episode's own sound work.

## Decisions this research supports

1. Choose a single physical rule that can generate different birth events. The breadth of techniques belongs inside that rule. A parade of brands, a timeline of logos or a generic cosmic creation montage would make the many requested references compete for attention.
2. Reserve the most elaborate design for the object the viewer is meant to inspect. Keep other areas quiet enough for very fast cutting. Speed increases the need for compositional hierarchy.
3. Make the historical mechanism become useful material in the present, and let the future concept alter a rule that has already been demonstrated. This can turn an AI-history checklist into a short story.
4. Use two speeds at once: very fast local transformations and a larger, easily understood progression. A short stillness can register as a shock inside this extremely fast music video; it need not make the episode feel slow.
5. Treat exact Chinese lettering, particle choreography, spatial camera work and material beauty as different production problems. Assign the method to the shot, then verify the actual image. An all-code or all-generated mandate would not follow from this benchmark.

The desired difference is an image-world with more sensuous matter, more dramatic scale and a more direct feeling of birth, while retaining the benchmark's discipline in hierarchy, sound ownership and recurrent visual meaning.
