# COMMON ROOM — cinematography

## The camera is another person in the room

The domestic camera watches two competent adults doing a practical thing. It neither diagnoses them nor recruits the audience to one side of the separation. Home register: held, slightly off-axis shared compositions with clear bodies and clear routes. Pauses belong to unfinished hand actions and obstructed objects. Their faces are visible whenever social presence matters; do not solve continuity by shooting backs and silhouettes throughout.

The postal hall briefly gives the camera the eye of a traveler arriving somewhere enormous. The architecture is not a villain's authority shot. Return from it to human working height.

## Formal rule: the composition persists while addresses change

Opening and ending each use a recognizable K-WIDE. The same lamp hangs over the same table in the same perspective. Kitchen-side door frames never change. What lies beyond them can be replaced between closed/open states. The audience measures the enormous departure by the small unchanged room.

Prefer the following authored operations:

1. Clean door closes to occlude its outside view.
2. Exact paper fold/tab insert shown in a readable object cut.
3. Return to the same kitchen setup with the destination changed.

This is an editorial state change, not a request that a video model fold a building while preserving every room. Keep all room pixels outside the threshold unchanged when making a narrow replacement. For different camera angles, use the approved empty room reference and inspect the same landmarks.

The houseletter insert need not show a continuous impossible fold. It does need to show one physical kitchen flap and two access tabs. Never cut from one flap to an image with two identical kitchens. Hand/body performances later belong to H3 only after separate motion-stage authorization; still proofs do not establish successful motion.

## Contrast without constant drift

The first large reveal is a cut from kitchen-scale handling to postal-scale space. The home's normal camera then holds still while characters move quickly within it. Compact-module testing uses shorter, tighter views and more physical obstruction; its rhythm comes from actions and sounds, not a camera shaking at every bump.

The decision resets all of that: a held wider frame, free counter length, no automatic zoom, hands stopped. The final kitchen resumes activity but stays in the open wide long enough for the shared obstruction to be understood. The hall is the rare scale break, not an excuse for five beauty shots.

Future motion may use **one modest lateral move in the hall** to reveal a missing room slot, if that reveals information absent from the starting frame. It is a proposed motivated move, not required for the still reel. K-WIDE stays locked for door comparisons. No pan across a flat picture; no 2–10% push applied by default; no comp move layered over a model move.

## Light, depth and faces

The high east window motivates the main domestic light. It lights the couple from camera-right and leaves softer shadow toward the western corner. Avoid contrast that hides either actor's eyes or ages them. The plain pendant makes a small warm table pool when switched on; it does not light every surface equally.

Use three planes when useful: table edge or a carton corner foreground; person and action middle; doorway/window as depth and source behind. Avoid pushing a foreground prop against the lens solely for a cinematic label. Both door worlds remain legible at K-WIDE without becoming more detailed than the action.

Faces are composed, mouths closed outside speech, small dark eyes without conspicuous highlights. Eda and Sen have ordinary even skin; never prompt pores, spots, severe creases or weathering. The physical behavior names where the eyes rest and what the hands do. No stunned, wistful, emotional, defiant, devastated, teary or knowing-smile instructions.

Future motion start frames show the first neutral performance state. One key action per generated shot; any essential transfer, close, open, release or fabric lift must be verified in the full take and again in the selected cut. Neither prompt nor attractive still proves it occurred.

## Frame and type safety

Master **16:9**, no decorative letterboxing. Maintain clean lower floor/table regions when possible for later English/Chinese subtitles, but never move a necessary hand action out of causal view merely to reserve a band. Keep critical objects away from the bottom12% of frame when an alternative framing preserves the action. Subtitle placement can shift for a necessary object insert. Exact text is added after image generation. All furniture surface labels that must survive future motion are finished in the approved start frame.

Do not use a framing crop that cuts shoes during a threshold crossing or removes the far edge of a module whose cramped size supplies the joke. Do not crop a wide hall test into a portrait to judge its scale.

## Review gates

- Full north-facing geography: Eda left/ring, Sen right/rain, sink left/hob right, one lamp, one table, two doors.
- Identity in edit order: age, skin, hair, eye size, shirt collar, body scale; individual similarity to a reference is insufficient.
- Object states: same cherry bowl and actual count, same pan and handle, one plan/kitchen flap, correct tab assignment, cloth/packed module positions.
- Silhouette at25% luminance: people, pan and door frames remain distinguishable. Check hands against counter and shirt values.
- Lighting: every dominant light has the named physical source; no unexplained doorway halo or glossy face.
- Spatial magic: outside view changes without interior drift, duplication or a third entrance.
- Causality: tray cannot fit the mini sink; pan safely fits the mini hob but leaves no lid/spoon work area; cloth is lifted off the hob before cooking; pattern weight changes from salt pot to spare cup in the final wide.
- Limits: a finished story reel validates staging, still continuity and audio/edit timing. Future generated motion still requires full playback review and action verification.
