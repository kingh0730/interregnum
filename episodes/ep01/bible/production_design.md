# COMMON ROOM — production design and room authority

## One invariant kitchen

The kitchen is physically singular. Every camera sees the same sink, lamp, worktop and bowl; moving an address never duplicates these. There are exactly two relevant entrances throughout. Their external destinations may change; their kitchen-side positions do not.

Room size for staging: **6.8m west–east × 5.0m north–south, ceiling 2.8m**. Coordinates below use southwest floor corner `(0,0)`; north/rear wall is `y=5.0`. Camera home is south, slightly east of the center, looking north. **Door assignment:** Eda always screen-left and west; Sen always screen-right and east in north-facing views. Both kitchen-side frames are plain ash, 1.0m wide and 2.35m high. Eda's door hinges at the west edge, Sen's at the east edge; leaves open away from the kitchen into their own vestibules. Neither has a special shared-room lock. Ordinary handles and each owner's lock exist on that owner's side. Closing a leaf does not stop its owner reopening it. Keep the kitchen threshold rectangular and unlit: no energy border, hologram or liquid surface.

**Worktop:** one continuous pale mineral slab, 3.5m long × 0.65m deep at 0.9m high, rear north wall between doors. Rectangular deep sink centered x2.05; simple tall curved tap. Single dark hob centered x4.65; modest flat extractor directly above it. The central clear run is roughly 1.6m and visually much more useful than either cooking module. Do not insert a refrigerator, microwave or second sink between shots. Cabinet fronts are ash with small dark pulls.

**Table:** ash rectangle 2.0m × 1.0m at x2.0–4.0/y2.1–3.1. Two chairs initially: Eda west, Sen east. During packing, one chair may be boxed/removed only if script continuity records it; the ending's vegetables occupy the sole available chair, not a spontaneously different room. Table north edge has a clear passage to the worktop. Neither door leaf collides with the table.

**Lamp:** one plain pale-yellow enamel inverted bowl, slightly tilted, at `(3.0,2.6,2.1m)`. It hangs above the table, never above the hob. Its asymmetry is mild and useful for continuity, not a visual gag demanding inserts. It remains through the ending.

**Shelves:** two short open ash shelves above the central counter, not a full cupboard wall. They remain background geography. The final script omits the shelf/stool disagreement; do not add it back through prop inserts or performance plans.

**Source light:** a high east-wall window at y2.4–3.7/sill1.9m provides broad daylight from camera-right. It is not a third doorway or a competing planetary view. Warm hanging lamp can be visibly on later; it creates a small pool on the table without flattening the rest of the room. Eda's red sky and Sen's rain are visible through doors with only localized secondary spill.

## Door states

- **EDA-0:** left doorway shows Eda’s packed old private room. This is the locked script opening state. The kitchen-side leaf and frame are unchanged when her address shifts to EDA-1. The postal hall first appears during her later departure, not in the opening kitchen threshold.
- **EDA-1:** same left doorway leads to Eda's ordinary bedroom vestibule, with a distant red planetary ring visible through its large far window. Keep a short real floor and bedroom threshold before the celestial view; nobody steps directly into space.
- **SEN-0 / SEN-1:** right doorway leads to Sen's old-home vestibule and a rain-dark covered landing. The endpoint gets clear evening rain beyond its sheltered floor. This is a safe ordinary entrance, not a precipice.
- **Both open:** the kitchen remains lit principally by its high east window/lamp. The exterior worlds can differ in time and weather without painting half of either face neon red or blue.

For every matched door-state change, preserve the exact kitchen plate outside the intended threshold region where technically possible. If a fresh generation changes cabinet lengths, door separations, table position or lamp, reject it. Do not accept plausible but different kitchens.

## Objects that carry causality

- **Sen's pan:** one dark carbon-steel frying pan,32cm diameter with long straight dark handle. It sits safely on the compact hotplate, but leaves no adjacent preparation space for its lid or spoon. The gag is missing work area, not an unsafe pan fit. The same pan reappears in Sen’s request for usable worktop space and at the final kitchen.
- **Compact modules:** two distinct FLOOR-STANDING usable counter units, each45cm wide ×45cm deep ×90cm high. One dull-white unit has a small practical sink; the other a safely usable18cm hotplate. Their feet stand on the floor near the north corners of the ash table, never on the tabletop. Full counter height and the visible cabinet/feet prevent a toy reading. Neither malfunctions: their limitation is almost no adjacent work area for two adults and ordinary cookware. During unpacking the hob is unplugged.
- **Wet tray:** plain rectangular dull-silver tray,42×30cm; width exceeds the mini sink. A few drops make a small wet patch on the nearby tabletop after the tray is set down. There is no spill on packed bedding in the locked script.
- **Packing:** two ordinary folded moving cartons and one tightly rolled off-white bedding bundle, kept below counter height. Enough for action, not a room full of inventory. Bicycle stays near Sen's east approach when required, with its exact position recorded per shot.
- **Tape measure:** one compact mustard-yellow housing, black strap. It can be absent or returned; do not generate several similar tools.
- **Houseletters:** two pale paper enclosures approximately A4 when closed, stiff enough to hold slits without looking metallic. Eda's has one ink-blue edge/thread marker; Sen's one clay edge/thread marker. One common kitchen flap receives two narrow address tabs through separate aligned slits. There is one flap, not two copies.
- **Paper plan:** a simple exact overhead plan with a single kitchen rectangle and two exterior address outlines. Diagram and exact lettering should be drawn/composited after generation using ordinary tools; never rely on invented legible glyphs. Keep letterlocking mechanically possible. No touchscreen.
- **Final cloth:** long pale unpatterned fabric with a charcoal paper pattern, laid across the worktop; lifts clear of the hob before cooking. No extractor-driven movement is required in the locked script. The pattern stays legible, with its weighted corner an exact continuity state. During the request state, a small stoneware salt pot weights its corner; the final wide replaces that weight with a spare cup and returns the salt to Sen. Cloth must not sit on an active burner. The final script keeps the right hob clear throughout; do not add an extractor-blown-pattern event absent from the locked script.

## Postal architecture

A sorting hall is a real public interior of exceptional scale, not a glowing computer. Broad pale structural piers rise beyond sight; high clerestory slots put discrete shapes of daylight on a dark stone floor. Building sections stand vertically in ordered, deep racks like thick folded letters: an apartment facade on one side, the pale blank backing of a closed letter on the other. Their thickness and shadows remain visible. A few narrow gaps reveal other occupied rooms. The shapes are architecturally plain: magnitude, proportion and repetition make them magnificent.

One clear floor route connects Eda to the hall's rack. A destination is understood by the shape of a slot and a small exact address tab, never a flying UI. The endpoint's missing bedroom is a clean absence within a building edge. Do not generate a fluid facade transformation. Author a before/after pair using one approved geometry plate and an ordinary match cut.
