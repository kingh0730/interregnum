# COMMON ROOM / 一室两家 — art direction

## Decision and approval status

**Approved direction: A, inhabited live action.** The story earns its impossible architecture by treating a pan and a door handle as ordinary physical things. Human faces, useful kitchen space and the enormous postal hall must belong to the same photographed world. Root inspected all six no-reference tests and approved A on2026-10-02. `style_A_scale` has substantially more palpable monumental space than B/C, whose sorting halls read as bookcase miniatures. `style_A_room` carries the ordinary adult presence the domestic scenes need. These are style approvals: its audition faces are not identity masters. Original test recipes are preserved in `episodes/ep01/build/lookdev_tests.json`; root owns the continuing production catalog `episodes/ep01/build/images.json`.

Public series branding is **AI SI - I**. Film title is **COMMON ROOM**, Chinese subtitle **一室两家**. Title design is an editorial overlay, never a fabricated wall sign.

## The three alternatives

### A — Inhabited live action

**Medium.** Photographed people and practical interiors; speculative architecture composed from physically convincing masonry, timber and folded building sections. Adult domestic comedy. Dry surfaces, clear moderate depth, no beauty campaign lighting. The planetary ring is natural distant geography, neither a hologram nor a glowing portal.

**Home register.** Chalk, ash wood and muted kitchen metal, practical side light, 32–50mm lenses. Faces carry age by proportion and comportment, with clear even skin; no caricature of middle age. Clothes are plain everyday cotton.

**Variation.** Close practical work can fill the frame; the kitchen's full width then releases it. The hall expands to 24mm monumental volume. Eda's far home introduces mineral red in the sky; Sen's landing carries slate rain. Those sources coexist only as framed views, with modest localized spill.

**Single visual departure.** A tiny human figure in an enormous postal nave, with apartment sections standing upright as folded letters. Scale, not ornate machinery, supplies the spectacle. Architecture originals remain Luma.

**Short style block.** "Photographic live-action frame; restrained adult speculative comedy. Dry natural surfaces, ordinary clear-skinned faces, motivated light, clear spatial depth, spare composition. The people and room feel physically built; only the connected spaces are impossible. No gloss, glow or ornamental clutter."

**Why choose it.** It preserves the small social observations and makes the last shared kitchen genuinely desirable. The postal reveal feels like a place one might inhabit. The medium leaves the joke to the blocking instead of announcing whimsy.

### B — Cut-colour 2D cinema

**Medium.** Fully flat, drawn 2D figures and environments: matte solid colour planes, confident charcoal-dark contours, limited value steps, no painted grain, dimensional render, gradients or reflective skin. Background architecture uses the same drawing grammar as faces.

**Home register.** Warm ivory, blue-black line, faded ultramarine clothing, clay shirt; broad unpatterned surfaces. Slightly elongated adult proportions and small dark eyes, no glossy animation eyes. Composition supplies scale through architectural silhouettes and negative space.

**Variation.** Kitchen uses broad horizontal planes; postal hall introduces thin vertical blades. The red ring is one giant rust arc across flat cream sky. The rain landing is dark indigo with a few discrete drawn drops. The two doors remain rectangular cuts into the composition, not painterly portals.

**Short style block.** "Fully flat hand-drawn 2D cinema, matte solid colour shapes and charcoal-dark contours, two or three value steps, adult human proportions. No gradients, reflections, painted grain, 3D render or paper border. Figures and architecture share one graphic medium."

**Why it could win.** The two door states and returning compositions can be absolutely legible, with replacement drawings an intentional final language. Risk: generic illustrative charm could blunt the adult awkwardness or turn the hall into a decorative poster. Reject if faces become cute or uniformly smiling.

### C — Constructed miniature cinema

**Medium.** An unmistakably photographed stop-motion world: paperboard architecture, matte painted wooden adult puppets, plain woven costumes, practical miniature lighting. Every person and room is constructed at the same scale; no live-action face pasted into a puppet body. No visible armature joints or decorative craft clutter.

**Home register.** Bone paperboard, muted clay, faded blue cloth, simple matte adult faces. The lens treats the miniature as inhabitable space; generous focus, no shallow toy-photography blur. A folded postal building is literally made from folded board, but does not flex or morph without a designed replacement state.

**Variation.** Kitchen is close and tangible. The hall's immense rack becomes a convincing constructed landscape, with deliberately sharp silhouettes. A painted red ring beyond Eda's miniature window belongs to the same miniature set.

**Short style block.** "Photographed stop-motion miniature cinema. Paperboard sets, matte painted wooden adult puppets and plain woven costumes, all one coherent constructed scale. Simple clear faces, practical side light, generous depth of field. No live-action skin, glossy toys, felt fuzz, visible joints or cute expressions."

**Why it could win.** Letterlocking becomes a material language with minimal explanatory work; authored replacements can look finished. Risk: the adult couple may read as dolls rather than people, and surface detail can overwhelm the joke. Reject if the kitchen resembles a dollhouse display rather than a room at human eye level.

## Palette meanings and the series copy

- Chalk / ash / dull silver: the common room and its useful, unclaimed capacity.
- Ink blue: Eda's clothing and address-thread edge; an identifier, not a moral temperature.
- Muted clay: Sen's clothing and address-thread edge; no red-versus-blue conflict symbolism.
- Mineral red: distant ring sky, restricted to Eda's view and a restrained edge reflection.
- Slate blue-grey: rain and outdoor depth through Sen's door.
- Pale yellow: the crooked domestic lamp, a small shared irritation that remains.

The episode's **copy** is the printed catalogue representation of the two compact cooking modules: perfect symmetry and frictionless even light are confined to that printed selling image. The actual delivered modules have ordinary materials and cast shadows. No human receives the copy's smoothness. The copy can appear only as a small carton insert if the script needs it; do not add a scene for it.

## Production method and asset policy

Prepare six independent Luma originals: one room/face test and one architectural scale test per direction. No reference images for this comparison. Inspect full frames and face crops before approving any face or location.

After selection, approve separate Luma base portraits for Eda and Sen; their appearance is deliberately ordinary, so no beauty exception is needed. Create one authoritative empty kitchen frame and compatible supplementary views. Each narrative original must reference that location and the relevant approved face(s)/prop(s). A later edit must not silently become the character or room master.

Spatial magic uses authored states, ordinary door closes, matched views, replacement images and decisive cuts. No geometry morph is required. Future H3 Max is reserved for approved natural body/hand performances after the user's separate motion-stage authorization. Do not add uniform zooms or panning over flat plates. This deliverable stops at the image-and-final-sound story reel.

The wardrobe in the tests is provisional but internally consistent: Eda ink-blue cotton overshirt over bone crew-neck top, charcoal straight trousers; Sen muted clay cotton shirt, dark olive straight trousers. Neither wears jewelry, hats, logos or glasses. Final garment details and face master IDs are locked after image inspection, not inferred from whichever test generated last.
