# COMMON ROOM — research and originality record

Development review: 2026-10-02. Series: **AI SI - I**. Working concept title: *One Door Each*.

The selected film follows two separating partners who can carry rooms between addresses but cannot copy their contents. They choose one generous shared kitchen over two cramped private facilities. The separation succeeds; shared use continues to cause ordinary inconvenience. This is invented material fantasy, not a forecast of transport technology or a claim about real physics.

## What the real practice contributes

Letterlocking supplies a concrete visual grammar: a sheet can carry information and form its own enclosure, and its folds and openings determine how it is accessed. The film transforms these relationships into rooms, address tabs and houseletters. It does not dramatize a historical correspondent or reproduce an archival letter.

| Detail verified in a primary source | Fictional transformation |
|---|---|
| The writing substrate can also become the enclosure. | The plan folds into the houseletter used to assign rooms. |
| A triangular corner wedge can pass through aligned vertical slits. | Address tabs visibly join the shared kitchen flap to two packets. |
| A working replica reveals a packet structure that is difficult to understand from a flat image. | The characters test the consequences of an allocation physically. |
| A slit begins and ends within the sheet; a cut enters from an edge. | Deliberate, readable differences between an access opening and a divided portion. |
| Locks can use attached material, detached material from the same sheet, or another substrate. | Tabs and fasteners have an intelligible construction rather than behaving as unexplained glowing symbols. |

The first three details come from Heather Wolfe and Jana Dambrogio's firsthand account, [“Let's make a model!” at the Folger Shakespeare Library](https://www.folger.edu/blogs/collation/lets-make-a-model-2/). The final two are defined by the research group's [Dictionary of Letterlocking: Manipulations](https://letterlocking.squarespace.com/manipulations). Both were independently opened during the concept review.

The initial pitch also cited [the virtual-unfolding paper in *Nature Communications*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21326-w). The independent review could not retrieve it because the publisher redirected to an inaccessible authorization page. The selected concept does not depend on a claim found only there. Source images are references for research, not assets licensed for use in the film; the production creates original paper objects and architecture.

## Nearby works and the intended difference

**Home Sweet Home (2026) is the most important comparison.** Its festival synopsis describes opposed decorating preferences, a literal split house, and a shared aesthetic reconciliation. *Common Room* keeps the households separate and preserves one uncopied useful room. The dramatic result must stay separation with negotiated common use, not a house-war compromise that restores the couple. [Festival synopsis](https://indieshortfest.com/2026/09/27/home-sweet-home-2/)

**Head Over Heels (2012)** already makes marital distance into domestic geometry: one spouse lives on the floor, the other on the ceiling. Its announced movement is toward putting the marriage back together. Our pair's new arrangement leaves them separated, with distinct addresses and incompatible daily tasks. [Cannes entry](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/head-over-heels/), [director's film upload](https://vimeo.com/54228768)

**Howl's Moving Castle (2004)** is an acknowledged adjacent work for nonlocal domestic doors. No claim is made that a doorway opening onto a distant world is itself new. The selected film's particular rule is that access can multiply while the room and its objects do not.

**Folding Beijing** is also relevant to the broader visual field, and was a much closer underlying-rule comparison for the rejected *False Unison* candidate. The published story explicitly allocates the same city to different populations at different times. *Common Room* has neither alternating populations nor time allocation. Avoid selling it as the discovery of folding cities. [Hao Jingfang's story, translated by Ken Liu, in *Uncanny*](https://www.uncannymagazine.com/article/folding-beijing-2/)

## Audience and production implications

The intended presentation is a complete 3–5 minute landscape short for speculative-fiction and independent-animation viewers, with English dialogue and English/Simplified Chinese subtitles. That is a production choice, not a researched demographic prediction.

YouTube's retention guidance emphasizes whether the first thirty seconds meet the title and thumbnail's promise; it also notes that replay peaks can reflect confusion. The opening should therefore show a door reassignment and one shared object whose state persists, rather than explain the postal system. Rewatching should reveal a relationship detail, not be required to understand the premise. [YouTube retention guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)

YouTube describes recommendation performance as dependent on viewer response and personalization rather than favoring one particular video format. This supports making a coherent short rather than reshaping it around an assumed universal feed formula. [YouTube search and discovery guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225)

The platform's independent-animation report documents an audience around creator-owned animation. It is promotional platform research, not a guarantee of demand for this episode or its production method. [YouTube independent-animation report](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/independent-animation-trends-report/)

The story can be proved before generated motion: reuse exact kitchen views, prepare distinct doorway states, and cut between authored states. The planned sound contrasts a familiar shared room with the different environments beyond its doors. Current production documentation supports reference-guided original images and separately produced voices/music; model capability alone is not evidence that a shot's geometry or performance succeeds. [Luma image API](https://fal.ai/models/luma/agent/uni-1/v1/max/api), [ElevenLabs voice-design documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/voices/voice-design/what-is-voice-design), [ElevenLabs Music API](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/elevenlabs/music/api)
