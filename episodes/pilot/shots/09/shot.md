# Shot 09 — TITLE: CONTINUITY

**Duration:** 4 s (0:50–0:54; abs 50.0–54.0)  **Tool:** JS (non-diegetic: a stroke beam on a black tube)

**Action:** Black. A beam writes the episode title in the Ministry's own letters, and each letter burns in: it blazes,
then decays to a dim afterglow that stays to the cut as a ghost. After the archive, the word reads three ways at once:
the Ministry's job, the editor's craft, and a machine that can only continue. The word lingers after it has gone,
which is the pilot's subject in one decay.
