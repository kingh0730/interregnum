# Production assets

Generative imagery uses the Codex built-in image generation tool. No Luma calls.

Official vectors: https://anthropic.com/press-kit (downloaded 2026-10-04, linked from https://www.anthropic.com/news). The unmodified `Claude Spark - Clay.svg`, `Anthropic symbol - Slate.svg` and ivory symbol are copied into `logos/` with shorter filenames. Trademarks remain Anthropic's. Generated plates leave text and branding blank; the vectors are composited afterward.

Fonts are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Noto SC files are the Simplified Chinese faces of Noto CJK, sourced from https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk. Lora, Poppins, Noto Sans Symbols 2 and Noto Sans Math are from https://github.com/google/fonts. Lora SemiBold is a static weight-600 instance of the upstream variable font. Local font files and license texts are in `../render_kit/fonts/`.

References: Opus v1 was rejected for short hair. Opus v2 extends both twin-tails to the floor without changing identity. Xiaoman v1 and environment v1 are the production references. Composite reference versions are exact vector overlays, not fresh image generations. The composite v3 and separate multi-angle hero plates were visually inspected and used for production.
