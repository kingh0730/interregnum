## Layer contract

Every final shot records absolute song time, frame interval, source asset hashes, pose cadence, camera model, alpha convention, foreground/subject/background order and colour space. Generated plates are not independently moved again if their camera is already moving. Camera motion is authored once. Preserve unclipped highlights until final finishing; use linear compositing for light, deliberate display-space type, and tag the eventual encode consistently.

Representative later proof: 3–5 seconds containing one clean face, one tile contact, one body reveal, one visible emptied socket and a paper trail that crosses behind then in front. Another proof uses full-body choreography with exact support. A third checks an actual named quartet with lyrics at phone size. Only then estimate and expand production. These tests answer genuine uncertainties; do not create a full speculative engine first.

## Comparison with the reference

The reference provides a stronger implemented procedural engine than this art-stage prototype. Our intended advance is character presence, material variety and shared depth across generated and authored elements. Nine stills establish a visual direction, not superiority to a finished music video. The later movie must earn that comparison through actual choreography, legibility and musical timing.
