The authoritative frame-count ledger is `build/timeline.json`; the authoritative planned occupancy ledger is `build/scene-state.json`. Both are plans, not an actual-image approval record.

## Rule

There are 23 seed-tiles in a single suspended fan, each with one sole-shaped indentation and one closed seam. Pressure on that indentation opens the tile once, turning it into one person; its empty oval socket stays. There are 22 contemporary AI people and one unlabelled future person. ## Supporting geometry

The fan's ribs and border are inert: they can support a shoe without activating. The 17.61 pause uses a narrow rib, never the reserved tile. Ordinary cloth, bodies, scenery, empty sockets and falling chips cannot create people. This must be legible from the sole-mark/seam motif, not explained in a caption.

Upward paper curls establish the air current at the first wide. Claude first makes a sling, then the group opens it into a canopy at 19.67–22.70. Wides show load-bearing lines from that canopy to waist sashes. Flight is the payoff of this visible mechanism. The final birth releases its gold frame; no bowl or pedestal may remain beneath the new person. Carry the new weight through a visible sag and recovery of the existing sail.

## Stable layout

Assign fan sockets clockwise 01–23 from the foreground hinge. Birth order follows the scene-state ledger. Reserve socket 23 at the opposite rim, distinguished by its gold frame. Keep screen travel left to right until the flight rise. Canopy attachment positions preserve the socket order; no person silently swaps sides between matching wides. Core pair foreground; secondary four middle; later ensemble farther along the arc, never made smaller as a different species.

The ancient theatre and night city are unfolding props on the fan's reverse; neither provides new seed-tiles. The future staircase is a suspended projection made from spent structural fragments, not walkable ground or a new source of bodies. Leave the reserved tile intact during the explosion.
