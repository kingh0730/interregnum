## Implications for the concept judge

- The strongest evidenced Mandarin hook is **human-readable physical personality**: recognition arrives before a caption is read. The July Gemini commentary/remix pair supports the existence of an audience for personified cross-model jokes; it does not prove any proposed film will go viral.
- The latest whale wave is useful as one immediate cultural signal. Do not base the whole visual direction on an existing whale-girl fan design. Original human silhouettes, original garments and a restrained recurring prop allow strong recognition without imitation.
- Historical/future concepts should change the film's rules: AlphaGo changes the floor, Transformer changes relationships, AGI changes transferable abilities, the singularity changes perspective. Avoid a lecture or a chronological slideshow.
- Suggested balance: three contemporary meme beats, two authored character beats, an ensemble passage, and one future discontinuity. This is a creative recommendation, not a quota.
- The prompt expressly requests named AIs and affectionate meme incorporation. Keep the jokes about staged temperaments; avoid factual allegations of incompetence, deceptive product tests or company endorsement.
- This research establishes no reliable AGI arrival date, no future capability guarantee and no evidence that all named systems are conscious. Forecasts belong to the film's speculative fiction.
