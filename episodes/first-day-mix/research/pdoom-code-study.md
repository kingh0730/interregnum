## What actually produces the sophistication

The important achievement is a composed sequence of **different spatial and temporal behaviors**, with typography acting inside those behaviors. Bloom alone is not the achievement. The implementation is a Three.js/WebGL2 compositor with Canvas2D typography, custom GLSL, authored lyric timing, offline audio analysis and deterministic offline export.

## What not to inherit

1. **An intentionally narrow medium is not this brief.** The shared ink/bone/orange vocabulary and recurring spark/mask are coherent for that film. Our brief explicitly needs photoreal, anime, ancient/modern, light/dark and variable cadence. Recoloring those plates would fail it. The strength to retain is persistent visual causality across different scenes.
2. **Technical imagery is not character acting.** The inspected scenes emphasize words, diagrams, procedural objects and a deliberately impersonal assistant mask. Our named human-like AI cast needs hands, gaze, gesture, vulnerability and funny practical behavior. A label plus a particle cloud does not satisfy “adorable human-like figures.”
3. **English text infrastructure is unsuitable unchanged.** `app/src/engine/lyrics.ts:120` normalizes to ASCII letters/numbers/parentheses; it would erase Chinese characters. `app/src/engine/type.ts` uses particular Latin font families and glyph/path layout assumptions. Chinese shaping, punctuation, fallback and character-safe timing must be designed independently. No reference font is approved by this study for reuse.
4. **The timeline is not a universal beat detector.** Its data includes separated stems and aligned words; `analysis/analyze.py:248` fits a grid to drum data and `:277` examines bar phase. Our supplied LRC has line starts only, and our current analysis uses the full mix. Equal spacing inside lines would invent vocal precision.
5. **Determinism has explicit costs.** Stateful adaptive sampling is rejected in `Engine.render`; the shoggoth entry is capped at 108 samples because silhouette noise would otherwise drive it to 324 (`app/src/timeline.ts:64` area). Choose small proof shots for shader cost and edge stability before committing to an effect across the video.
6. **Source analysis is not proof of a superior finished film.** Camera moves, staging, charm and music sync must be reviewed in the eventual render. The code demonstrates mechanisms, not audience response.

## Original implementation targets

These are design proposals, not claims of completed production capabilities.

- **One object, multiple physical laws.** A small common object carried by the cast survives each medium change in the same screen position and orientation: photographed cloth → painted cel → folded paper → light scattering → cloth again. Use matched camera/prop coordinates, segmentation masks and a short authored crossover. Every transformation must have an identifiable start, contact and result.
- **A real foreground crossing.** Render foreground obstructions, character and background separately, with shared camera transforms and depth-aware particles. At least one particle trail must pass behind a hand and in front of a torso, with matching contact light. This makes hybrid depth verifiable in a still frame.
- **Multiple clocks within one shot.** Export at 60 fps; selected drawn character poses update at 12 fps, props at 24 fps and camera/particles at 60 fps. A later full-frame 12 fps passage should commit completely to that medium. Never let random generated intermediate faces simulate “animation.”
- **Character-specific consequences.** A code action should reveal temperament: a cautious hand checks a result, an impatient character jumps ahead, a meticulous character repairs a tiny wrong seam. A two-stage anticipation/contact/settle animation reads as intention where beat-driven scaling reads as a sticker.
- **Chinese lettering as material, sparingly.** Reconstruct clean text using a licensed Chinese typeface and manually approved shape treatment. Embroidered, cut-paper, carved or phosphor lettering belongs to its environment. Name captions get a clear reading interval and one dominant information plane; lyrics do not compete with simultaneous dense labels.
- **Three registers of spectacle.** Macro tactile event; medium character interaction; wide spatial impossibility. Move among them deliberately. A 0.5-second stillness before a large event can create more perceived speed than adding ten more particles per frame.
- **A future scene that exceeds its frame.** Use a perspective portal with a foreground lip, cast shadows and an asymmetrical camera pass. The destination should be a new shared space the cast can occupy, not an infinite central tunnel. Distinguish AGI/SGI/singularity as speculative ideas in the writing, not an authoritative forecast.

## Proof requirements for later production

Before a motion batch, prove a short shot containing a held face, hand/prop contact, foreground crossing and medium change; inspect source and composite at full resolution. Render the same timestamp twice to test determinism only if using a new renderer. Inspect transition endpoints and neighboring frames for identity and geometry; normal-speed review remains required. Test Chinese titles at phone size. The audio remains the supplied master and edits follow its actual duration. This stage is research/art direction; no final motion or copied reference assets are authorized by this document.
