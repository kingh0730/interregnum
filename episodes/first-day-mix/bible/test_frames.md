**Direction C selected:** tactile photographed mechanisms and beautiful humanlike leads, with complete cel, paper, pixel and woodblock bodies. White daylight is the home register; the articulated night theatre supplies the dark counter-register. The choice is supported by actual frames, not model ranking.

Nine built-in image-generation calls were completed in this pass: six independent A/B/C explorations, two focused developments, and one night-geometry edit. ## C5 night geometry refinement

Selected night reference: `work/first-day-mix/art/c5.png`. Actual image inspection: the reflective continuous floor is gone; near and midground sockets visibly expose black air; thin bronze brackets connect them to the fan; both foreground faces, costumes and hand connection remain recognizable. The giant fan, warm lamps and cobalt plume retain their hierarchy. Most incidental balcony figures have been removed; printed figures remain part of the artwork. Some lower fan panels and scaffolding were simplified in the edit, so C4 remains the richer mechanism source. Foreground sockets have become thicker/larger, and the total is not the planned 23-slot layout. This is a substantial improvement in spatial legibility and a selected design reference, not a dimensioned geography master.

## Selection scope

Art direction is settled; production assets are not approved. These tests answer whether the medium contrast, light, composition and mechanism are promising. They do not prove births, dance, actual support contacts, 22-character occupancy, name readability or motion. Small smiles may fit the festive performance, but open-mouthed singing is not implied; final poses must match the composed behaviour plans.

The six-character ensemble tests deliberately isolate style interaction. Do not copy their cast count into a 23-person production wide. Build separate clean character masters and a dimensioned fan/canopy layout before final frames.

## Rejected shortcuts

Do not fill negative space with mountains, symbols or fog. Do not accept a beautiful floor when the story requires a hole. Do not hide the support behind particles. Do not call an individual attractive image continuity-safe. Do not use mismatched small cartoon bodies to satisfy an all-adult cast design.
