# Art direction: contact changes the medium

## Three candidates

**A — Porcelain rehearsal.** Entire world photographed as handcrafted miniature performance: ceramic Go stones, paper clothing, tiny humanlike adult figures, hard morning light. Strong physical clarity and charm; risk of toy-commercial sameness across 44 seconds.

**B — Ink under acceleration.** Complete flat cel worlds: ivory paper, cobalt contour, peach spot colour; held 12 fps character poses over independently smooth 60 fps curves and debris. Strong readable staging; risk that scale and medium variety feel smaller than the brief.

**C — Contact cinema.** Photographic humanlike AI performers pass through sculptural printing mechanisms into full cel, woodblock, pixel and folded-paper worlds. One physical contact drives each conversion. This is the intended hybrid: A supplies weight, B supplies silhouette, C supplies the formal rule. Test frames are exploratory designs, not six ready-to-animate keyframes. Final choice and findings live in `bible/test_frames.md`.

## Image hierarchy

Every frame has one action readable at 320 pixels wide. Complexity accumulates behind or around that action. One elaborate focal machine is allowed; surrounding architecture is spacious. Keep faces, wrists and shoe silhouettes clean. Particles must have an origin, directional force, occlusion and an end state: fragments return as the next surface, rather than becoming a generic sparkle layer.

Colour is conserved across medium changes: ChatGPT celadon/ivory, DeepSeek cobalt/ivory, Claude apricot/ink, 豆包 persimmon/cream, Gemini violet/cyan, Grok black/silver. Other cast use smaller discrete colour patches, never a full rainbow fog. Daylight carries birth and first contact; night is a brief paper-city set powered by its actual signs. Ancient imagery is a theatrical scroll stage, not a claim that AI existed in antiquity.

## The copy

The one frictionless image is the pristine unused oval floor, a promise of a perfectly completed world. Each awakened surface gains a seam, a hand, a specific body and a new obligation. Photographic performers retain clear natural skin and physically motivated light; no waxed faces. Beauty should invite affection, not become a fashion advertisement.

## Six exploratory frames

A1 birth on Go stones; A2 ensemble airborne. B1 birth on Go stones; B2 ensemble airborne. C1 first cross-medium contact; C2 final airborne ensemble. Generate each independently to compare direction, not to infer continuity. Inspect action, depth, charm, silhouette, medium commitment, elegance and feasibility. Do not score prompt intent as image evidence.

## Production routing

Exploratory designs use the approved Codex imagination workflow. That does not change final routing: Luma originals for scenes and environments; built-in Codex for beauty-focused base faces, elaborate focal mechanisms meeting both attention/detail criteria, and generative edits. Exact labels, diagrams, pixel geometry, emission layers and transitions are authored. Preserve clean references; avoid serial repainting.

## Style blocks for production

A: Photographed handmade miniature theatre. Porcelain, folded paper, crisp visible construction. One directional morning source; deep focus and clear physical contact. Restrained humanlike faces. No glossy toy packaging, margins or text.

B: Flat 2D cel animation, matte ink, simple clean faces, restrained eyes, confident cobalt contour, ivory field and apricot spot colour. No gradients on figures, no 3D-render shading. Dramatic foreshortening through drawn perspective, not a flat collage.

C: One fully photographic plane meets one fully drawn plane along a sharp physical contact boundary. Maintain legible body silhouettes and common directional light. The converting object retains outline and momentum; the rest of the world does not dissolve. No all-over glitch, neon mist or painterly half-real face.

## Final choice after images

Select C. C3 is the daylight canopy/material reference; C5 is the night fan/void reference. A supplies ceramic/paper tactility and B supplies clean contour, but neither can carry the brief alone. The selected pair offers genuinely different light and scale while preserving ivory/cobalt and a shared articulated mechanism. Keep the actual-image limitations in `test_frames.md`; no exploratory image becomes a 23-person geography master by being selected for design.

Performance exception for this festive music video: occasional small natural smiles are acceptable. Avoid broad open-mouth smiles as automatic generation defaults; singing mouths still require an intentional later lip-sync plan. Warmth primarily comes from helping, balancing and catching.
