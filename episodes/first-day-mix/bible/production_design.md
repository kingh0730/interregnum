## Set and prop state

Start with a black Go stone above a suspended fan containing 23 marked ivory seed-tiles. The Go stone is a historical prop, not a seed or a cast member. Stones have actual thickness and curved undersides. A 19×19 line pattern appears only as an overhead historical motif, not a functional board suspended under every later scene. Transformer attention uses visibly crossing paths between six panels; it is a visual analogy, not a literal model diagram.

The transforming ground is never an endless floor. Every wide must show its perimeter and the void. Pressure on a sole-print opens a seed’s closed seam, consuming that support and adding one occupant. The empty socket remains visible; no birth from arbitrary material. The fan’s inert ribs can be stood on. The canopy is folded from Claude’s already-awake clothing and catches the established updraft. New settings are explicitly folded out by performers; no unexplained teleportation. Record all occupants, including those behind the camera, before generating each wide.

A marked final oval stays asleep through 30.68. It may carry speculative future labels in screen space, never as a product identity: AGI / 通用智能; ASI / 超级智能; SGI? / 含义未定; 奇点 / 假想边界. The research note explains that SGI has no single stable meaning. These are questions, not historical achievements or a forecast date.
