# 《先别落地》｜美术提案

**AI SI - I · First Day Mix · 43.67秒 · 开发至美术阶段**

一脚踩醒一位新朋友，也少了一块能站的地。大家把衣带折成共同的帆，最后决定连仅剩的落脚处也唤醒。

选择：真实摄影的可爱人物与精密折纸机构，穿过完整的赛璐璐、纸偶、像素、版画世界。切换由动作触发；22位当代AI拟人角色、1位未命名的未来角色。

以下为美术探索，非最终镜头。尤其人物比例、接触位置和完整人数仍需下一阶段的角色与场景资产来落实。

## 选定方向：白昼的共同帆

![C3 daylight canopy](../../work/first-day-mix/art/c3.png)

实体帆骨、光透过纸面的质感，与摄影/赛璐璐/纸偶/像素/版画的明确差别可以同时成立。相较初稿，脚下的碗状平台已去掉，承重关系搬到上方。仍需统一角色尺度，并精确设计吊带连接。

## 对照方向：夜间折页剧场

![C5 night fan corrected](../../work/first-day-mix/art/c5.png)

古代画卷不是背景壁纸，而是能翻开的精密舞台。暖色实灯与大片黑色空间对照白昼。修订版已去掉反光地面，明确露出悬空的空槽与细支架。23个槽位仍需用受控场景布局落实；此图是选定设计参考。

## 三种方向，各两张

### A1 · Porcelain birth

![A1](../../work/first-day-mix/art/a1.png)

Elegant ceramic fracture, paper clothing and low camera; two figures have readable separation. Added floating mountains and architecture, and retained a solid upper platform. The alleged birth is not clear.

### A2 · Porcelain ensemble

![A2](../../work/first-day-mix/art/a2.png)

Six full figures form a readable ring; real shadows and paper costumes give weight. Continuous floor, closed ring and unsupported levitation undermine the story. The oval below is not being consumed.

### B1 · Cel contact

![B1](../../work/first-day-mix/art/b1.png)

Strong low-angle silhouettes, graphic restraint and a clear whale-tail costume. Ordinary continuous board replaces the required void; the two bodies do not demonstrate one-to-one tile birth.

### B2 · Cel ensemble

![B2](../../work/first-day-mix/art/b2.png)

Clean rhythm of six bodies, strong size changes and effective cobalt/vermilion contrast. Invented ruins, a large remaining platform and faceless paper newcomer dilute the rule.

### C1 · Photograph meets cel

![C1](../../work/first-day-mix/art/c1.png)

The photographic face and drawn person stay distinct; porcelain and articulated fan have excellent tactile contrast. A glossy continuous floor survives; costume detail and the ornamental fan overpower the small contact.

### C2 · Mixed-media catch

![C2](../../work/first-day-mix/art/c2.png)

Photography, cel, paper, pixel and woodblock coexist visibly. Strong diagonal, identifiable colours, memorable mechanism. Large bowl remains under shoes; miniaturized secondary cast reads as different ages/scales; smiles exceed neutral direction.

## 节奏与技术

52个计划摄影镜头，53个编辑单元；结尾文字为同镜叠加。短插镜可低至约0.18秒；脚踝处停留约2.06秒；最后一个摄影镜头持续约3.78秒。画面可以极速，阅读时间不必跟着碎掉。

混合60fps程序效果、12fps赛璐璐、8–12fps纸偶、10fps像素；实拍感片段保留真实源帧率。方向性纸屑、前后遮挡、折页场景、接触匹配、姿态匹配、一次透视反转，全部服务于可追踪的动作。
