# Delivery review

## Verified or inspected

- The exact 43.670-second source WAV and its PCM hash were established before production.
- All 69 planned shot units cover frames 0–2620 without a gap or overlap. The edit remains 60 fps.
- The complete 720p assembly rendered all 2621 frames with no missing assets. Its contact sheet was inspected across the whole duration.
- Source-frame and sampled action reviews cover all selected generated motion sources. The raised-hand/chest/heel phrase, bun transfer, skid, felt breath, fingertip touch, flight and brush contact were checked in sampled frames. This does not claim full-speed motion approval.
- Foot-ring anchors, foreground hand timing, clipped aerial limbs, floor reflection occlusion, pencil visibility, glyph contrast and the no-shadow AGI image were corrected after those reviews.
- Required Chinese/Latin display glyphs are present in the bundled fonts; see `font-checks.json`.
- Arbitrary-seek tests pass the documented GPU raster numerical tolerance. The last eleven frames are exactly static in the render. The strict hash result and measured byte differences are retained rather than described as bit-identical GPU seeking.
- Master source-code/config hashes are in `master-input-hashes.json`. The production build is frozen during export. Final codec, dimensions, frame count, colour tags, duration and PCM comparison are recorded in `delivery-checks.json` after muxing.

## Explicit limitations

Full-speed musicality, naturalness of all choreography and the side-by-side artistic comparison with p(doom) remain unverified by a human viewer/listener. They are not marked passed. The supplied line-level lyric anchors are used; no invented measured syllable alignment or BPM is claimed. Full-mix onset proximity is recorded in `transient-audit.json`, not presented as a certified drum grid.

The film uses the planner's authored and cutout alternatives where generated performance or framing failed. Camera motion on cutouts is limited. Individual 0.17–0.18-second roll-call cuts show portions of a continuous phrase; they cannot each contain a complete readable three-second dance. Six lead entrance names meet the required duration independently.

The broad roster is an ensemble and expandable set of Easter eggs, not a claim of an exhaustive current AI market census. SGI is deliberately ambiguous; it is not renamed ASI or presented as an established successor to AGI.

The viewing MP4 uses AAC. Only the PCM master is intended for bit-identical decoded audio comparison with the supplied WAV. Neither deliverable has been published or sent to another person.
