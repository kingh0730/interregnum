When an idea is clearly good by reasoning (e.g. giving Luma front, three-quarter and profile refs instead of one
portrait), adopt it into the process directly. Don't spin up a test to confirm it.

**Why:** King (2026-09-30) stopped a cheap multi-angle-ref test: "stop. don't need to test this. it's a good idea just
by reasoning." After many rounds of image tests he wanted momentum, not more verification.

**How to apply:** test only when the outcome is genuinely uncertain and the answer changes what we do (a model choice, a
prompt style with a real trade-off). Otherwise write the idea into the playbook and move on.
