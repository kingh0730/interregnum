# Image generation as an imagination and camera partner

King approved remembering this methodology on 2026-10-02. Use Codex's built-in image generation for
exploration, particularly its appealing camera angles and interesting visual presentation.

- Separate **design invention** (what the thing could be) from **visual interpretation** (how to see an existing
  thing compellingly). The latter can explore camera position/height/angle, framing, perspective, lighting,
  scale cues and staging without changing the underlying design.
- Establish purpose, constraints and fixed features. Inspect the generated proposal, identify its substantive
  contribution, develop useful discoveries in language, then refine. Retain a written candidate; image-first
  is optional. Do not confuse polished rendering or added decoration with a better idea.
- When exploring a view, retain approved identities, designs, location geometry and entity states. Check the
  actual angle, visibility and adjacent cuts for continuity; an attractive view must not silently change props
  or erase people.
- This exploratory role uses Codex without prerequisite Luma retries. Final production still follows existing
  routing: Luma original-image default with established exceptions; Codex generative-edit default. Exploration
  does not automatically become a production master. No Luma imagination comparison has established superiority.
- Evidence is mixed: King preferred hybrid in the first two cases; the expanded 12-brief study's assistant
  ratings found no clear overall advantage. Camera-angle value is King's adopted preference, not a separately
  controlled result. Use small purposeful explorations under existing budget rules, not mandatory batches.
